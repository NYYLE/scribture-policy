Privacy Policy for Scribture

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Overview

Scribture ("the app") is a Bible study app for reading Scripture and writing, drawing and highlighting beside it. This privacy policy explains how your data is handled when you use Scribture. By using Scribture, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

Data Storage

Everything you create in Scribture — including handwriting and drawings, highlights, bookmarks, text and sticky notes, shapes, images you add to pages, and your settings — is stored on your device. Scribture has no account system and no servers of its own. Scribture does not upload, share or have any access to your personal data, and never sends it to any server, cloud service or third party.

The Bible translations in the app (the World English Bible and the Berean Standard Bible) are built into the app, so reading them does not use the internet.

Please be aware that while we designed Scribture to keep your data local and private, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure or reliable. We cannot guarantee the absolute security or integrity of data stored on your device. We strongly recommend making regular backups using the built-in export feature.

Data Backup

Scribture includes an optional export and import feature that saves your notes, drawings, highlights, bookmarks, page images and settings as an encrypted backup file. You choose where the file is saved, and Scribture never transmits, uploads or sends it anywhere. If you choose to save a backup to a cloud folder (for example, Google Drive) through your device's file picker, that copy is handled by the provider you chose under their own privacy policy. Importing a backup replaces the data currently in the app. We are not responsible for any loss of data resulting from device failure, accidental deletion, or any other cause.

Separately, Android's own device backup may include Scribture's data if you have turned on backup in your device settings. This backup is run by Android and Google, not by Scribture, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn it off at any time in your device's settings.

Sharing and Copying

When you choose to copy verses, share verses or share a page as an image, Scribture hands the text or image to the app you pick through Android's standard share menu or clipboard. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and Scribture does not see what the receiving app does with it.

In-App Purchases

Scribture offers a one-time in-app purchase (Scribture Pro) processed entirely through Google Play. Scribture does not collect, store, or have access to any payment or billing information. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your Google account. For details on how Google handles your purchase data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Scribture does not ask you for any permissions while you use it. When installed, it holds the following permissions, which are needed only to talk to Google Play for the Scribture Pro purchase:

  • Google Play Billing — to offer and confirm the Scribture Pro purchase.
  • Internet and network state — used only by Google Play Billing to reach Google Play. Scribture itself does not connect to the internet.

Images you add to a page are chosen with Android's photo picker, and backup files with Android's file picker, so Scribture only ever sees the individual items you pick. Scribture does not request access to your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive permission.

Third Party Services

Scribture uses the following third party services, each of which operates under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing — for processing the one-time Scribture Pro purchase. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services — standard Android platform services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Beyond the above, Scribture does not integrate with any analytics platforms, advertising networks, crash reporting services or other data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Scribture is intended for users aged 13 and over. Scribture does not knowingly collect, transmit, or share any personal data from any user, including children. Anything entered into the app by any user — regardless of age — is stored on their device and is not sent anywhere by Scribture. We recommend that users under 18 use the app with parental awareness. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's use of Scribture, please contact us using the details below.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can erase or delete individual items within the app, clear a whole page with the eraser's "Clear everything on this page" option, or uninstall Scribture to remove all of its data from your device. Backup files you exported are kept wherever you saved them until you delete them. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or for legal reasons. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Scribture after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
